Interpretation Guide

Correlation Range: -1 to +1

Strong (±0.7 to ±1.0): Variables move together significantly

Moderate (±0.4 to ±0.7): Some relationship between variables

Weak (0 to ±0.4): Little to no relationship

Positive: Variables increase together

Negative: One increases while the other decreases
